across `seal-host`, `mcp-seal-dev` and the shipped artifacts, in one place,
ranked.

**Why ranked this way.** The first tier is not the most severe. It is the tier
that makes every other fix *verifiable*. Right now CI can pass while skipping
the entire core, so anything closed below tier 1 is closed on trust. That is the
exact failure this project keeps hitting: a control that exists but does not
run. Fix the instruments first, then the defects they measure.
